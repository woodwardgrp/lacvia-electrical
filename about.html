About Lacvia Electrical

Hey, I’m the person behind Lacvia Electrical — and by “person,” I mean just me! I'm a one-man army based right here in Swansea, offering friendly, reliable electrical services to homes and small businesses in the area.

I’m a recently qualified electrician, and while I may be new on paper, I bring fresh training, up-to-date knowledge, and a serious commitment to doing things properly. I started Lacvia because I believe electrical work doesn’t have to be stressful or full of jargon. When I show up, I want my customers to feel relaxed — like they’ve got a mate taking care of things, not just another tradesman rushing through the job.

I take pride in every job, big or small, and I always aim to leave your space safer, tidier, and better than I found it. Whether it’s fixing a dodgy socket or helping you plan a full rewire, I’ll talk you through it in plain English and make sure you're comfortable every step of the way.

If you're in Swansea and looking for an honest, hardworking spark who genuinely cares about the work — and the people — behind each job, I’d be proud to help.

Oh, and if you’re wondering how to pronounce “Lacvia” — it’s *Lace-vee-uh*. It’s a blend of my daughters’ names, Lacey and Olivia. Not a Latvian phone brand, I promise!
